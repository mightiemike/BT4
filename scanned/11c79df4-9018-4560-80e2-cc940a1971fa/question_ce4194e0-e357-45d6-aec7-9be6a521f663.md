Preconditions: sequencer configured with fork activation heights that gate Prague-only opcodes/precompiles at a specific L2 height per citrea_primitives::forks::fork_from_block_number, used elsewhere in runner.rs. Impact (Critical, DETERMINISM/EXECUTION_AGREEMENT): a transaction admitted as valid by the mempool's chain_spec view can execute under different semantics in the STF, risking two honest nodes (one validating admission, one executing) diverging on whether/how the tx applies.
