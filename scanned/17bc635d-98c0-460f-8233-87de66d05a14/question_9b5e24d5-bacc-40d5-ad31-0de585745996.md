Can a miner who is a minority stake holder submit a TenureChangeCause::BlockFound tenure-change block whose previous_tenure_blocks field is computed against a tenure-extend chain that differs between two nodes because one node received a since-orphaned sibling block that inflated its locally observed tenure_len, so that check_nakamoto_tenure returns Some(..) on one node and None on the other for byte-identical tenure-change transactions?
