# Q1920: check-confidence via collateral-remove-redeem: absorb a sub-step failure into a fold flag and proceed on 

## Question
Does `collateral-remove-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1211) let an unprivileged attacker who controls `amount` used for BOTH the collateral removal and the share redemption reach `check-confidence` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:305) in a state where it absorb a sub-step failure into a fold flag and proceed on partial state?
