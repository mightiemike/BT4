## Question
Does `collateral-remove` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1107) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the `ft` trait principal reach `write-feeds` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:149) in a state where it consume a cache entry after the vault it describes has already moved? - Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - protocol insolvency through uncollateralised debt
