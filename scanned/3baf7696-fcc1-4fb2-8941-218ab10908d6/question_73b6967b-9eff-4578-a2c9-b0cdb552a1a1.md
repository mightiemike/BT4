Preconditions: regtest, sequencer's DepositDataMempool freshly started. Binding: number of times a given real Bitcoin `moveTx` output is minted as cBTC == 1 (exactly once). Impact: Critical, CUSTODY — replayed/duplicated deposit minting or permanently blocked legitimate deposit.
