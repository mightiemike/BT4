Target: `DepositDataMempool::remove_deposits` using `calc_tx_id` recomputed independently from `fetch_deposits`'s returned deposits (deposit_data_mempool.rs). Preconditions: `A` legitimate, already accepted in mempool; attacker submits `B` afterward attempting displacement (per Q1) or, alternately, after `A` is included and removed via `remove_deposits(&[A])`, submits `B` which independently satisfies `calc_tx_id(B) == calc_tx_id(A)` yet was never executed on Bitcoin. Binding: cBTC credited on L2 == a Bitcoin-side deposit that actually happened, counted exactly once — broken because `B`'s legitimate deposit is discarded without credit and without possibility of resubmission (its txid is fully consumed by `A`'s inclusion). Impact: Critical, funds permanently frozen for `B`'s depositor.
