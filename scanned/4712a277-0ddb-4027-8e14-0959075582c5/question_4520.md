## Question
Does `liquidate-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1604) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the borrower targeted reach `accrue` (mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:835) in a state where it strand value on the market contract when a later step of a composite call fails?
