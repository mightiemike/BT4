In crates/light-client-prover/src/circuit/mod.rs process_complete_proof(), the batch proof method id used for `Z::verify(proof, &batch_proof_method_id.into(), ...)` is selected via binary_search_by_key on `batch_proof_output_last_l2_height` against the JMT-stored `batch_proof_method_ids: Vec<(height, method_id)>`, but `batch_proof_output_last_l2_height` is read from the untrusted `batch_proof_output` extracted from the proof itself (via Z::extract_raw_output before verification).
