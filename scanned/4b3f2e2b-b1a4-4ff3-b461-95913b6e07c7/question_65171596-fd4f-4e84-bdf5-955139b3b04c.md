NakamotoTenureDownloader::try_accept_tenure_blocks checks `block.header.consensus_hash != self.tenure_id_consensus_hash` per block but never re-validates that self.tenure_id_consensus_hash itself still corresponds to the sortition winner recorded in the node's live SortitionDB at the time the blocks are finally accepted (there can be an arbitrary delay between downloader construction in make_tenure_downloaders and try_accept_tenure_blocks completing). Equality broken: TENURE/REWARD — each block's tenure must equal its authorising sortition's *current* winner, not the winner recorded when the download started. Impact: Critical — blocks from an orphaned tenure get delivered to the relayer as if canonical, risking their acceptance into chainstate and a subsequent state-root mismatch against peers that correctly discarded the orphaned tenure.
