Target: `NakamotoChainState::make_scheduled_miner_reward` (tenure.rs) coinbase recipient selection. An attacker who wins a sortition submits a Nakamoto coinbase tx `TransactionPayload::Coinbase(buf, Some(recipient), Some(vrf_proof))` where `recipient` is an arbitrary principal unrelated to the miner's origin address. Preconditions: epoch_id >= Epoch21, valid VRF proof matching the tenure's sortition per `check_normal_coinbase_tx`. Call sequence: `make_scheduled_miner_reward` sets `recipient = recipient_opt.cloned().unwrap_or(miner_addr...)` unconditionally, with no check that recipient is authorized by the miner's stacking/consensus role. Equality: reward recipient == the sortition winner's own address unless intentionally delegated; here `recipient` is attacker-chosen with no validation that it's a legitimate delegation. Impact: High (mis-payment bounded to a single tenure's coinbase+fees, not a fork/root break, since this is by design in 2.1 pay-to-alt-recipient). Proof idea: integration test where a miner sets an arbitrary third-party principal as coinbase recipient, confirm the reward posts to that address only once and totals coinbase+parent_fees.
