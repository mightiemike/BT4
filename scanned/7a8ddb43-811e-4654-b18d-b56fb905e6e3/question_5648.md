## Question
Does `supply-collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1175) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the `ft` trait principal deciding which vault is routed to reach `remove-user-collateral` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-market-vault.clar:205) in a state where it pass a health check and then change, in the same transaction, the quantity it checked? - Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - protocol insolvency through uncollateralised debt
