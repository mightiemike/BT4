# Q5432: find-asset via liquidate: absorb a sub-step failure into a fold flag and proceed on 

## Question
Does `liquidate` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1382) let an unprivileged attacker who controls `borrower`, any third-party principal reach `find-asset` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:584) in a state where it absorb a sub-step failure into a fold flag and proceed on partial state?
