Preconditions: attacker can call `citrea_sendRawDepositTransaction` repeatedly with distinct but simulation-passing blobs. Binding: cBTC credited by SYSTEM_SIGNER == a Bitcoin move-to-vault output counted once, and every honestly presigned output must eventually be counted; indefinite queue-jumping breaks 'eventually counted'. Impact: Critical if it becomes permanent (not merely delayed) — check whether `fetch_deposits` retains FIFO fairness or can be starved by unbounded resubmission of order-independent deposits, freezing a specific user's funds indefinitely.
