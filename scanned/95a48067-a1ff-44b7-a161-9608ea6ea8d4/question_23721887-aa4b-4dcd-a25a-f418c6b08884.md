Preconditions: same as above; batch prover splits proof into chunks + one signed Aggregate blob. Binding: authority that authorized the *chunk content* == authority (`batch_prover_da_public_key`) that authorized the *Aggregate* referencing it — these are checked independently and the equality is never enforced for chunks.
