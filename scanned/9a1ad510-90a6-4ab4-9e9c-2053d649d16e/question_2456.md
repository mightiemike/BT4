## Question
Does `collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1020) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the position's existing collateral and debt composition reach `debt-remove-scaled` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-market-vault.clar:473) in a state where it strand value on the market contract when a later step of a composite call fails?
