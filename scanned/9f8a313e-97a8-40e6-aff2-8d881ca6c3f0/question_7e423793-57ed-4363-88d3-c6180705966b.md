DepositDataMempool::fetch_deposits(limit_per_block) (crates/sequencer/src/deposit_data_mempool.rs) is non-mutating and idempotent by design (comment states 'does not remove'), meaning it can be called multiple times per block-production attempt (e.g. once during a dry run and once during actual production) and will return the identical Vec<Deposit> both times as long as remove_deposits hasn't run in between.
