In accounts.rs::calculate_miner_reward, when poison_reporter_opt is Some(reporter_address) and participant.miner is true, the reward branch computes StacksChainState::poison_microblock_commission(coinbase_reward) and sends it to reporter_address while the remainder is 'destroyed'. Can an attacker craft a PoisonMicroblock report timed so that it is processed against a *different* participant's MinerPaymentSchedule row than the one actually punished (e.g. exploiting a stale or reused vtxindex/height lookup in get_poison_microblock_report), so that coinbase_reward computed and paid to the reporter derives from a different, larger tenure's coinbase than the offending miner's own? Preconditions: two different tenures at adjacent heights with different coinbase sizes; a poison report keyed only by height u32. Call sequence: get_poison_microblock_report(height) -> find_mature_miner_rewards binds report to matured MinerPaymentSchedule at that height -> calculate_miner_reward substitutes coinbase_reward from participant, which may not equal the punished miner's own schedule if height collides across forks. Equality broken: commission paid == fraction of the *offending* miner's own coinbase, not another participant's. Impact: Critical, reward mispayment inflating attacker profit beyond the offense. Proof idea: two-fork test with distinct coinbase values at colliding heights, submit report, assert paid commission matches the coinbase of the specific participant whose keys verify the double-sign, not any other schedule row at that height.
