Target: `check_normal_coinbase_tx` (nakamoto/mod.rs) VRF-sortition_hash binding versus reward recipient. An attacker who is the legitimate sortition winner nonetheless submits a coinbase whose `vrf_proof` verifies against `sortition_hash` from a *different*, older sortition that also elected them previously (VRF proof reuse across two of the attacker's own wins). Preconditions: attacker has won two distinct sortitions with the same VRF keypair. Call sequence: `check_normal_coinbase_tx` only checks `VRF::verify(leader_vrf_key, vrf_proof, sortition_hash.as_bytes())` for the *current* block's sortition_hash argument passed in by the caller; if the caller ever passes a stale sortition_hash (e.g., due to fork-handling reuse of a cached hash), a proof from an unrelated tenure validates. Equality: tenure T's coinbase VRF proof must attest to T's own sortition_hash, i.e., tenure==its authorising sortition; any mismatch breaks TENURE/REWARD equality and lets the wrong tenure's coinbase mature. Impact: Critical, reward paid to a tenure that isn't authorized by its claimed sortition. Proof idea: integration test calling `check_normal_coinbase_tx` with a valid VRF proof but a sortition_hash from a prior, different sortition of the same miner, and confirm it is rejected in the full block-append path, not just the unit check.
