Preconditions: a coinbase-height boundary near genesis or a tenure with zero streamed fees so `is_parent()`/`is_child()` predicates are satisfied by unrelated rows. Call sequence: `find_mature_miner_rewards` -> `insert_matured_parent_miner_reward`/`insert_matured_child_miner_reward` -> later `get_matured_miner_payment` -> `try_add_parent`. Equality that breaks: reward credited to the child == coinbase(child) + confirmed fees(child) + produced fees(that child's own parent), not an unrelated parent's produced fees. Impact: Critical, STX credited exceeds coinbase-plus-fees for the tenure. Proof idea: unit test constructing two `MinerReward` values with mismatched lineage but matching `is_parent`/`is_child` predicates and asserting `try_add_parent` incorrectly combines them into a `total()` exceeding the true coinbase+fees for that tenure.
