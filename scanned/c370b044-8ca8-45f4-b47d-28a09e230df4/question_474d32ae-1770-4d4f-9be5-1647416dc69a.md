Binding: StorageKey::singleton_owned(Prefix::from_slice(k1)) != StorageKey::singleton_owned(Prefix::from_slice(k2)) for any k1 from ChunkAccessor's key space and k2 from any other accessor's key space, for all attacker-chosen wtxid inputs.
