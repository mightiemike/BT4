# Q5065: interpolate-rate via collateral-remove: bind a balance before an accrual and assert against it aft

## Question

- Exploit idea: `interpolate-rate` interpolates between packed u16 curve points. Reach it through `collateral-remove` and bind a balance before an accrual and assert against it after.
- Invariant to test: the state a safety check approved is the state the money movement executes against
- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of user funds at rest or in motion
- Fast validation: In `local-testing/tests` on a local fork, drive `collateral-remove` with the set of assets held, then read `interpolate-rate` state before and after in the same block and assert the two sides of the invariant are equal.
