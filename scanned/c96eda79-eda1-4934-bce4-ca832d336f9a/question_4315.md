# Q4315: accrue-and-cache via collateral-remove: pass a health check and then change, in the same transacti

## Question
`accrue-and-cache` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:245) keys `index-cache` on `{timestamp: stacks-block-time, aid}` and returns the cached record forever after, with no invalidation when the vault later moves. Can an unprivileged caller of `collateral-remove` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1107), by choosing the `price-feeds` buffers, use that to pass a health check and then change, in the same transaction, the quantity it checked, violating the invariant that every second of elapsed time is charged exactly once, to one index, in one direction and producing direct theft of another user's collateral?

- Entrypoint: `collateral-remove` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1107`), unprivileged and publicly callable

Reach it through `collateral-remove` and pass a health check and then change, in the same transaction, the quantity it checked.

- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of another user's collateral
