Target: `StacksChainState::get_coinbase_reward` (stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/db/blocks.rs) delegating to `epoch.coinbase_reward(mainnet, first_burn_block_height, burn_block_height)`, then `calculate_scheduled_tenure_reward`'s `coinbase_at_block.saturating_add(accumulated_rewards)`. Attacker action: attacker triggers a burnchain reorg that changes `chain_tip_burn_header_height` for an already-scheduled-but-not-yet-matured reward, such that when the block is *re-processed* after the reorg (same consensus_hash/block_hash, different burn height context), `get_coinbase_reward` computes a different `coinbase_at_block` than the original schedule, and the *matured* payout later uses whichever `MinerPaymentSchedule.coinbase` was last written, potentially exceeding the coinbase constant for the block's true, canonical burn height. Preconditions: a burnchain-side reorg not deep enough to invalidate the Stacks block but altering its recorded burn height context. Equality that must hold: `MinerPaymentSchedule.coinbase` == `get_coinbase_reward` evaluated at the block's *canonical* burn height, fixed and immutable once scheduled, never re-derived to a higher value after a burnchain reorg. Impact: Critical, coinbase inflation beyond the epoch-defined constant for that height. Proof idea: integration test replaying the same Stacks block after a shallow burnchain reorg that shifts `chain_tip_burn_header_height`, asserting the stored `coinbase` field in `payments` is idempotent and never overwritten with a larger value.
