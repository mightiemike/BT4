## Question
Does `liquidate-multi` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1593) let an unprivileged attacker who controls how many entries share one price snapshot (price-feeds is passed as none) reach `socialize-debt-asset` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:879) in a state where it consume a cache entry after the vault it describes has already moved?
