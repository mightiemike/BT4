## Question
Does `liquidate` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1382) let an unprivileged attacker who controls which collateral and debt asset pair is targeted reach `next-index` (mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:379) in a state where it consume a cache entry after the vault it describes has already moved?
