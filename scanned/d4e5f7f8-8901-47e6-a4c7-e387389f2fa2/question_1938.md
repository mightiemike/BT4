# Q1938: calc-liq-debt-repay via liquidate-redeem: bind a balance before an accrual and assert against it aft

## Question
Entering through `liquidate-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1604) while controlling the vault whose share price the redemption moves, can an unprivileged attacker make `calc-liq-debt-repay` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:723) bind a balance before an accrual and assert against it after? `calc-liq-debt-repay` takes the liquidation factor times the debt with `mul-bps-down`, so the invariant that the state a safety check approved is the state the money movement executes against would fail, yielding permanent freezing of funds.

- Exploit idea: `calc-liq-debt-repay` takes the liquidation factor times the debt with `mul-bps-down`. Reach it through `liquidate-redeem` and bind a balance before an accrual and assert against it after.
- Invariant to test: the state a safety check approved is the state the money movement executes against
