Target: check_normal_coinbase_tx (nakamoto/mod.rs) validating the coinbase tx's VRF proof field against the tenure's sortition-winning VRF seed. Attacker action: attacker who is a minority miner wins their own legitimate sortition on a competing burn fork, then attempts to submit that tenure's coinbase+blocks onto the majority fork by reusing the same VRF proof, hoping check_normal_coinbase_tx does not bind the VRF proof to the specific sortition consensus_hash of the fork being extended. Preconditions: attacker has a valid VRF proof from a real (minority-fork) sortition win with their own BTC. Call sequence: attacker submits block on majority fork citing a VRF proof from a different sortition -> check_normal_coinbase_tx validates VRF proof format -> if it does not check proof-to-consensus_hash binding, tenure is scheduled -> make_scheduled_miner_reward credits attacker on the majority fork despite not winning majority fork's sortition. Equality: the tenure ID (sortition consensus_hash) credited by make_scheduled_miner_reward on fork F == the consensus_hash of the sortition whose VRF proof and burn-weight computation actually won on fork F, not a proof imported from a different sortition/fork. Impact: Critical, reward paid to a non-winner on the canonical fork; also a block-acceptance failure (invalid block accepted network-wide) if the check is genuinely missing. Proof idea: Rust test submitting a block on fork F with a VRF proof drawn from a legitimate win on fork F', asserting check_normal_coinbase_tx / block validation rejects it and no MinerPaymentSchedule row for fork F is ever created for that miner.
