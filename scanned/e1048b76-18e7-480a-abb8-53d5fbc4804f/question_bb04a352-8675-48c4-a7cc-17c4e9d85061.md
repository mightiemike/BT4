[stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/db/transactions.rs handle_poison_microblock] Can an attacker report a poison-microblock at height H where `microblock_height_opt` returned by `get_microblock_pubkey_hash_height` corresponds to a *different* miner's pubkey-hash than the one actually double-signing, causing the wrong miner's coinbase (stored via `insert_miner_payment_schedule`) to be slashed? Preconditions: two distinct miners A and B each with distinct `mblock_pubkey_hash` registered at different heights within `MINER_REWARD_MATURITY` of the current height. Call sequence: craft `PoisonMicroblock(header_1, header_2)` both signed under B's key, submit as a report; verify `mblock_pubk_height` resolved by `get_microblock_pubkey_hash_height(&pubkh)` maps deterministically to B, not A. Equality: `pubkh` recovered from header_1/header_2 == the pubkey hash actually registered by the slashed miner's block at `mblock_pubk_height`, and reward paid == coinbase of that exact miner's scheduled `MinerPaymentSchedule`. Scoped impact: Critical, wrong-party reward slashing/theft. Proof idea: integration test asserting `calculate_miner_reward`'s `child_address` after punishment equals the pubkey-hash owner recovered by `check_microblock_header_signer`, never a different miner.
