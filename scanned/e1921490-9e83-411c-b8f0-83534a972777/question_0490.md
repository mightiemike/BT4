## Question

- Attacker controls: a pre-funded but undeployed UEA address derived from attacker-chosen universal-account fields
- Exploit idea: Cause `MigrateUniversalTx` to trigger an unsafe state-transition edge case, so it can reuse signed authorization material in a different chain or owner context.
