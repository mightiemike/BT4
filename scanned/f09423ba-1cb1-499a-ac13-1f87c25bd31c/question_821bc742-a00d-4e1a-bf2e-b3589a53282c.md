`nakamoto_keys::coinbase_height(ch)` and `nakamoto_keys::ongoing_tenure_coinbase_height(coinbase_height)` are both written per-tenure in `advance_tip` and used by `calculate_scheduled_tenure_reward`/`get_matured_miner_reward_schedules` to find `matured_coinbase_height = coinbase_height - MINER_REWARD_MATURITY` and its tenure header. Can an attacker submit a tenure-extend block (`TenureChangeCause::Extended*`) crafted so that no new `coinbase_height` MARF write occurs for that burn view, yet a competing fork's block at the *same* burn view is a `BlockFound` tenure-change that does write a fresh `coinbase_height`, so that `get_coinbase_height(tip, ch)` returns `None` on one fork and `Some(n)` on the other for logically the same consensus hash `ch`, causing `get_matured_miner_reward_schedules` to pay the coinbase reward on one fork but error/skip it on the other — breaking TENURE/REWARD (reward paid once == coinbase+fees earned)? Impact: Critical, reward double-payment or unclaimed-reward loss across a reorg.
