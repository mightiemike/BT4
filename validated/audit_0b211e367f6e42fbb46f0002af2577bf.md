There is no "component count/index" style broken invariant analogous to `jas_image_readcmpt` reachable via a trusted-schema binary or ProtoJSON parse path in this codebase — Go's runtime also converts any such indexing bug into a recoverable panic rather than a memory-safety violation, which the scan rules explicitly instruct to reject as a generic panic rather than a genuine analog.
