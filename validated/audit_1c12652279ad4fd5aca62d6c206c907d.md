### Title
Relayer fee is paid unconditionally, independent of whether the emulated transfer action succeeds - (File: `runtime/near-wallet-contract/implementation/wallet-contract/src/lib.rs`)

### Summary
The reported bug class is a missing "check the outcome of a value-moving external call before treating it as successful" issue: `BribeVault.transferBribes()` used `token.transfer()` (whose boolean return value is never checked) instead of `safeTransfer`, so a non-standard token silently failing left the vault's bookkeeping out of sync with what actually moved. The closest reachable analog in this nearcore codebase is in the Wallet Contract's `rlp_execute` flow, where the relayer's compensation ("fee") is paid out as an independent, unconditional promise that is not linked to the success of the transfer/ERC-20-emulated action it is supposed to compensate for.

### Finding Description
In `inner_rlp_execute` (`runtime/near-wallet-contract/implementation/wallet-contract/src/lib.rs`), when the parsed transaction kind is `EOABaseTokenTransfer` or `ERC20Transfer` with a non-zero `fee`, the contract immediately spawns a standalone promise batch that transfers the fee to the relayer (`context.predecessor_account_id`): [1](#0-0) 

This fee-payment promise is created as a sibling receipt, separate from the promise chain that actually performs the user's intended transfer/ERC-20 emulation (`action_to_promise(...).then(ext.rlp_execute_callback(...))`, or for ERC-20 transfers the `storage_balance_of` → `ft_transfer` chain in `nep_141_storage_balance_callback`): [2](#0-1) 

The only place the wallet contract checks the outcome of the actual transfer is `rlp_execute_callback`, which inspects `env::promise_result(0)` and refunds the *user's attached deposit* (not the fee) on failure: [3](#0-2) 

Because the fee-transfer promise is dispatched before—and independently of—the callback that verifies whether the underlying `ft_transfer`/native transfer actually succeeded, the relayer is paid regardless of whether the requested value transfer (native or NEP-141/ERC-20 emulated) completes. This mirrors the root cause of the reported issue: a value-moving step (`token.transfer`/fee payment) is executed without gating it on the success of the operation it is meant to correspond to, so failures (e.g., NEP-141 token panics on insufficient balance, excessive `storage_deposit` cost, non-standard token semantics, or gas exhaustion during `ft_transfer`) do not prevent the payout.

### Impact Explanation
A relayer can collect the fee attached to a signed Ethereum-style transaction (native or ERC-20 emulated) even when the corresponding transfer never lands—for example if the wallet's NEAR balance is insufficient to cover both the transfer and the `storage_deposit`/gas needs of a non-trivial NEP-141 token, or if the token panics for any reason after `storage_balance_of` succeeds. This is unauthorized value movement out of the user's wallet: the user pays the relayer for work that was not completed, and the wallet contract has no mechanism to reclaim or condition the fee on the outcome of the transfer it nominally compensates.

### Likelihood Explanation
This is reachable by any relayer (an unprivileged submitter of a `rlp_execute` transaction/access-key holder) simply by relaying a transaction with a non-zero fee whose target action is likely to fail (e.g., pointing at a token contract known to panic under certain conditions, or crafting a transfer amount that will exceed available NEAR after storage costs). No special privileges beyond the FunctionCall access key already granted to relayers are required, and the condition depends only on the relayer's/user's own transaction construction, making it practically triggerable.

### Recommendation
Chain the fee-payment promise so it only executes after the underlying transfer/ERC-20 action succeeds (e.g., attach it via `.then()` off the same promise chain as `rlp_execute_callback`, or move the fee transfer into `rlp_execute_callback`'s `PromiseResult::Successful` branch), rather than dispatching it eagerly and unconditionally in `inner_rlp_execute`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Relayer submits an `rlp_execute` transaction wrapping a signed Ethereum ERC-20 `transfer` call with a non-zero `fee`, targeting a NEP-141 token contract.
2. `inner_rlp_execute` immediately creates and dispatches the fee-transfer promise to the relayer (`runtime/near-wallet-contract/implementation/wallet-contract/src/lib.rs:381-384`).
3. The chained `storage_balance_of` → `ft_transfer` promise subsequently fails (e.g., token panics, insufficient gas/balance for `storage_deposit`).
4. `rlp_execute_callback` reports `success: false` for the transfer, but the fee promise dispatched in step 2 has already succeeded independently—the relayer keeps the fee despite the failed transfer.
