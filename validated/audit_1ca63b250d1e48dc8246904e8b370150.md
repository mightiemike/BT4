## Finding

The rounding logic in `AllocateTokens` (x/uvalidator/abci.go) creates a genuine, unbounded accounting drift between the distribution module's recorded liabilities and its actual bank balance, driven purely by ordinary fee traffic.

### Title
Boost-reward truncation in `AllocateTokens` under-funds `x/distribution` relative to credited `ValidatorOutstandingRewards`, causing progressive conservation-of-value drift - (File: x/uvalidator/abci.go)

### Summary
`AllocateTokens` computes each active Universal Validator's boost reward as a full-precision `DecCoins` amount and credits it directly to the validator via `k.DistributionKeeper.AllocateTokensToValidator(ctx, validator, reward)` [1](#0-0) , but only transfers the *floored integer* sum of those rewards (`extraCoins`) into the `distribution` module account [2](#0-1) . The truncated fractional remainder (`extraChange`) is not used to back the UV-specific liability that was just recorded — instead it is folded into `remainingFinal` and routed back to the fee collector to be redistributed by the *standard* distribution `BeginBlocker` to all validators proportionally by normal (unboosted) power [3](#0-2) .

### Finding Description
In vanilla `x/distribution`, `AllocateTokensToValidator` is always paired with moving the *exact* (un-truncated, i.e. the full collected) amount into the module account first, and any decimal remainder is tracked internally by the module's own `FeePool.CommunityPool` DecCoins accumulator, so ledger and balance always reconcile. Here, `x/uvalidator` calls the lower-level `AllocateTokensToValidator` directly with a full-precision reward, but transfers to `distribution` only `extraCoins = floor(sum of rewards)`. The difference (`extraChange`, up to just under 1 unit per fee denom) is never delivered to `distribution` as backing for the specific `ValidatorOutstandingRewards` entries that were just incremented; it is instead delivered later, generically, to different validators via the vanilla flow. This breaks the implicit invariant that every `AllocateTokensToValidator` credit must be backed 1:1 by an equal transfer into the module account, since the recorded liabilities (`allocated`, full precision) now permanently exceed the value physically moved to back them (`extraCoins`), by `extraChange`, every single block.

### Impact Explanation
This is a genuine, per-block, non-self-healing drift, not a one-time truncation dust: each block the sum of validators' claimable `OutstandingRewards` in the `distribution` module grows slightly faster than the module's real bank balance. Over time this compounds into `distribution` module insolvency, at which point `MsgWithdrawValidatorCommission` / `MsgWithdrawDelegatorReward` transactions begin failing with insufficient-funds errors as the module cannot pay out what its own ledger says it owes. This is an accounting-corruption impact ("corruption of ... gas fee accounting") explicitly listed as in scope, reachable purely through ordinary fee-paying transactions with no privileged access required.

### Likelihood Explanation
The drift occurs automatically on every block where at least one active UV signs and fees are nonzero — it requires no crafted exploit, just ordinary chain operation. An attacker can accelerate the effect by paying transaction fees using many distinct low-value denominations (if the fee/ante configuration accepts multi-denom fees), since `extraChange` can accrue up to nearly 1 unit *per denom* per block, maximizing the unbacked liability generated per block.

### Recommendation
Either (a) transfer the full, un-truncated `allocated` DecCoins amount (rounded up, not down, or reconciled against `feesCollectedInt` precisely) into the `distribution` module before/along with each `AllocateTokensToValidator` call, or (b) route `extraChange` back into `distribution`'s own `FeePool.CommunityPool` (or an equivalent dec-remainder tracker) rather than mixing it back into `remainingFinal` for redistribution to unrelated validators, so that recorded liabilities and transferred token amounts always match exactly.

### Proof of Concept
A block-level integration test that: (1) registers several active UVs, (2) collects fees in multiple distinct denoms with fractional-remainder-inducing power ratios (e.g., UV power fractions that don't divide evenly into the fee amounts), (3) runs `BeginBlocker`, and (4) asserts `sum(ValidatorOutstandingRewards for boosted UVs)` against the `distribution` module's actual bank balance — will show the ledger total permanently exceeds the bank balance by the `extraChange` amount, repeating and compounding every subsequent block.
