### Title
Wallet Contract Double-Pays Relayer Fee Plus Full Deposit Refund on Failed Cross-Contract Call - ([File: runtime/near-wallet-contract/implementation/wallet-contract/src/lib.rs])

### Summary
The `near-wallet-contract` (the eth-implicit-account "Wallet Contract" that emulates Ethereum EOAs on NEAR) forwards a relayer `fee` immediately and unconditionally when processing an emulated base-token/ERC-20 transfer, and separately tracks the *full* originally attached deposit (including that same fee) to refund to the caller if the downstream promise fails. Because the fee is not subtracted from the tracked refund amount, a relayer who deliberately (or opportunistically) causes the forwarded action to fail receives both the fee payment and a full refund of the entire attached deposit, draining the wallet's own NEAR balance by the fee amount on every such failure.

### Finding Description
`inner_rlp_execute` builds a `CallerDeposit` that snapshots the entire attached deposit for later refund: [1](#0-0) 

For an `EOABaseTokenTransfer`/`ERC20Transfer` with a non-zero `fee`, the contract immediately fires a separate transfer promise sending `fee` to `context.predecessor_account_id` — unconditionally, before the outcome of the main action promise is known: [2](#0-1) 

If the main action promise later fails, `rlp_execute_callback` refunds the *entire* `CallerDeposit.yocto_near` (the full original attached deposit, which already includes the `fee` that was separately sent moments earlier) back to the same `predecessor_account_id`: [3](#0-2) 

`CallerDeposit::new` never subtracts the `fee` from `context.attached_deposit` before recording `yocto_near`, and `rlp_execute_callback` has no knowledge of the fee already paid out, so there is no verification that "amount refunded" + "amount already forwarded as fee" stays within the amount that was actually deposited by the caller. This is the same root cause as the reported bug class: a value that is tracked/refunded (`yocto_near`/"tokenAmount") is not reconciled against an already-executed, independent transfer of a sub-amount (`fee`/"bridgeAmount") drawn from the same funds, so on the fallback/failure path more value is paid out than was ever deposited.

### Impact Explanation
Any relayer (an unprivileged account that calls `rlp_execute` on behalf of a user, or any other predecessor submitting an RLP-encoded emulated transfer with a non-zero fee) can cause the main forwarded action to fail — e.g., by supplying insufficient attached gas for the target `FunctionCall`/`Transfer`, or targeting an account/contract state that will make the promise fail — and receive: (1) the `fee` sent immediately by `inner_rlp_execute`, and (2) the full attached-deposit refund (which already contains that same `fee`) via `rlp_execute_callback`. The excess `fee` amount is paid from the wallet contract's own NEAR balance (the eth-implicit account's funds), constituting unauthorized value movement out of the account. Every failed fee-bearing relayed transaction leaks the fee amount from the wallet's balance to the relayer, which can be repeated to drain the account.

### Likelihood Explanation
Triggering this requires only a single, unprivileged `rlp_execute` call with a crafted RLP transaction specifying a non-zero `fee` and an action engineered to fail on the destination (e.g., low gas, invalid receiver, or a target contract that reverts) — fully reachable by any relayer/meta-transaction sender without special privileges, and repeatable at will since each failed relayed transfer leaks the fee.

### Recommendation
Subtract the already-forwarded `fee` from the amount tracked in `CallerDeposit` before use as the failure-refund amount (i.e., `CallerDeposit::new` should use `attached_deposit - fee`, or `rlp_execute_callback` should be passed the post-fee amount), so total payout to the predecessor on failure never exceeds what was actually deposited. Alternatively, defer sending the `fee` promise until after the main action's outcome is known, or make the refund path aware of and subtract any fee already disbursed for the same call.

### Proof of Concept
1. Relayer (predecessor) calls `rlp_execute` on the victim's eth-implicit wallet contract, attaching deposit `D = amount + fee` for an `EOABaseTokenTransfer`/`ERC20Transfer` with `fee > 0`.
2. `inner_rlp_execute` immediately creates a promise sending `fee` to the relayer (`predecessor_account_id`) at [4](#0-3) , and separately records `CallerDeposit{ account_id: predecessor_account_id, yocto_near: D }` (the full deposit, fee included) per [1](#0-0) .
3. The relayer crafts the transfer's `gas`/target so the main forwarded promise fails (e.g., insufficient gas on a `FunctionCall`, or a target that rejects the transfer).
4. `rlp_execute_callback` observes `PromiseResult::Failed` and refunds the full `D` back to the relayer at [3](#0-2) .
5. Net result: relayer received `fee` (step 2) + `D` (step 4) = `D + fee`, while only `D` was ever attached — the extra `fee` is paid out of the wallet contract's own balance, repeatable on every failed fee-bearing transaction.
