### Title
Redeem reverts with `ERR-INSUFFICIENT-ASSETS` because `assets` is a stale cache that excludes accrued-but-uncollected interest, freezing depositor funds until a borrower repays - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar`)

### Summary
`redeem()` gates the payout against the raw `assets` data-var, but the share price used to compute that payout (`convert-to-assets-preview`) is derived from `total-assets-preview`, which additionally includes interest that has accrued on outstanding debt but has not yet been physically repaid into the vault. Because `assets` is only increased when `system-repay` runs, it is a cached value that is never invalidated when its source (`total-debt`/interest via `next-index`) moves forward every block. When a depositor tries to redeem shares whose value now includes this uncollected interest, `inkind` (the computed payout) exceeds the stale `current-assets`, and the `asserts!` guard reverts the whole transaction, exactly mirroring the `xERC4626` `storedTotalAssets` underflow class of bug (there it underflowed on subtraction; here Clarity's checked arithmetic instead surfaces the same root cause as a hard revert via the explicit guard).

### Finding Description
`redeem()` in `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar` binds: [1](#0-0) 

`current-assets` is `(var-get assets)` — a book-keeping value that only tracks principal deposits/repayments, and is increased solely inside `system-repay` via `(var-set assets (+ (var-get assets) interest-paid))`: [2](#0-1) 

`inkind` however is `convert-to-assets-preview(amount)`, which uses `total-assets-preview`: [3](#0-2) 

`total-assets-preview` adds `interest = debt-preview - total-borrowed` on top of `current-assets`, where `debt-preview` grows every block through `next-index`, independent of any repayment: [4](#0-3) 

So the "source" that determines share value (`total-debt`/interest, driven by `next-index`) moves continuously, but the cached value used as the withdrawal ceiling (`assets`) is only updated by the unrelated, permissioned `system-repay` call. `convert-to-assets-preview` also divides by `total-supply-preview`, which adds a not-yet-minted `treasury-lp-preview` reserve-fee dilution; when `fee-reserve` is low (including its default value of `u0`), this dilution is negligible, so `inkind` tracks `total-assets-preview` almost 1:1 and will exceed `current-assets` as soon as any interest has accrued and not yet been repaid.

### Impact Explanation
Any depositor (in the simplest case, the sole depositor holding effectively all shares) who waits for interest to accrue and then calls `redeem()` for their full position will have `inkind > current-assets`, causing the `(asserts! (>= current-assets inkind) ERR-INSUFFICIENT-ASSETS)` guard to revert the entire transaction. The user cannot withdraw any part of their principal-plus-accrued-value until a borrower voluntarily calls `system-repay` and pushes enough `interest-paid` into `assets` to close the gap — an event the depositor cannot trigger or predict. This is a temporary freezing of funds (the withdrawable share value, including unclaimed yield) purely due to a stale cached accounting variable, not user error or insufficient real liquidity elsewhere in the pool. The same pattern is duplicated verbatim across every vault contract in `mainnet/contracts/vault/` (`v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`), multiplying the blast radius.

### Likelihood Explanation
No malicious action or DAO compromise is required — any organic passage of time with outstanding, un-repaid debt produces the accrued/uncollected interest gap. The guard is hit as soon as `total-assets-preview - current-assets` (uncollected interest) exceeds the treasury-fee dilution introduced by `total-supply-preview`, which is minimal or zero whenever `fee-reserve` is set low (a normal DAO parameter, not a compromise). The scenario is most acute for early/sole depositors of a vault, or any depositor attempting a full redemption, making it readily reachable in normal operation.

### Recommendation
Do not gate `redeem`/`withdraw` payouts against the stale `assets` book-value; gate against the actually available underlying liquidity (`get-available-assets`, i.e., real token balance backing withdrawals) instead, and/or realize interest into `assets` (or an equivalent accrued-interest counter) atomically as part of `accrue()`/`next-index` progression rather than only on `system-repay`. This keeps the cached liquidity ceiling in sync with the value used for share pricing, eliminating spurious reverts on legitimate redemptions while still properly limiting withdrawal to real collectible liquidity.

### Proof of Concept
1. Vault is initialized with `fee-reserve` at or near `u0` (a normal, DAO-set operating parameter).
2. Alice deposits `amount` into the vault via `deposit()`; she is issued shares 1:1, and `assets` is incremented by `amount`. [5](#0-4) 
3. The vault lends part of the deposit out via `system-borrow`, and interest accrues over subsequent blocks purely through `next-index` recalculating `total-debt`/`debt-preview`, with no `system-repay` occurring. [6](#0-5) 
4. Alice calls `redeem()` for all her shares. `current-assets` still equals her original deposit (unchanged since step 2), while `inkind = convert-to-assets-preview(amount)` now includes the accrued, uncollected interest, making `inkind > current-assets`.
5. The `(asserts! (>= current-assets inkind) ERR-INSUFFICIENT-ASSETS)` check at line 808 fails and the transaction reverts, even though the protocol's own accounting says her shares are worth more than what she is trying to withdraw — she is frozen out of her funds until an unrelated borrower repays.

(define-public (system-repay (amount uint))
  (let (
        (states (var-get pause-states))
        (u (try! (accrue)))
        (scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (debt (total-debt))
        (total-borrowed-amount (var-get total-borrowed))
        (capped-amount (if (> amount debt) debt amount))
        (principal-reduction (calc-principal-ratio-reduction capped-amount scaled-principal debt))
        (capped-reduction (if (> principal-reduction scaled-principal) scaled-principal principal-reduction))
        (updated-scaled-principal (- scaled-principal capped-reduction))
        (principal-repaid (mul-div-down capped-amount total-borrowed-amount debt))
        (interest-paid (- capped-amount principal-repaid))
        (total-borrowed-new (if (> total-borrowed-amount principal-repaid) (- total-borrowed-amount principal-repaid) u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (not (get repay states)) ERR-PAUSED)
    (asserts! (> amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (try! (receive-underlying capped-amount tx-sender))
    (var-set principal-scaled updated-scaled-principal)
    (var-set total-borrowed total-borrowed-new)
