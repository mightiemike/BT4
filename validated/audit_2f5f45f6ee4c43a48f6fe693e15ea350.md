Since integer addition is associative/commutative, and the cross-slot/cross-thread accumulator is a `u128` with enormous headroom (up to `u128::MAX`), the storage/slot iteration order (including the intentionally-randomized `AccountStoragesOrderer::with_random_order`) cannot change the final sum or cause a divergent panic outcome between honest nodes — every node processes the exact same set of stored accounts (just in different order), and the sum is order-independent modulo overflow. The only place a real `u64` overflow could occur is the per-slot local sum at line 5764-5766, but the total lamport supply in the system (genesis + inflation) is enforced elsewhere to remain far below `u64::MAX`, and an unprivileged attacker cannot mint lamports out of thin air — all SVM-level transfers conserve total lamports (enforced by system-program/transaction balance checks), so no combination of user-controlled small-lamport accounts can push the true total, or any single-slot subset of it, anywhere near `u64::MAX`. Because the total is bounded by real economic supply rather than by attacker-chosen values, this path is not reachable by an unprivileged user who only creates/resizes/closes/rewrites accounts they already fund, and there is no order-dependent divergence risk since addition here is commutative/associative within the enforced supply bound.
