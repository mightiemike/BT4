The reported bug class is a quadratic-voting exploit specific to Gitcoin's Allo V2 `QVBaseStrategy`, where payouts are proportional to `sqrt(voice_credits)` summed per recipient — a concave function where `sqrt(a) + sqrt(b) > sqrt(a+b)`, making vote-splitting across recipients profitable.

nearcore has no analogous concave/square-root value-distribution mechanism reachable by an unprivileged transaction signer. The relevant reward/stake-weighted computations in nearcore are all linear in stake:
- `RewardCalculator::calculate_reward` distributes validator rewards strictly proportional to `stake / total_stake` with a linear uptime multiplier [1](#0-0) .
- Validator/chunk-producer selection samples proportional to stake via a weighted index, not a concave transform of stake [2](#0-1) .
- The economics spec explicitly documents this as a linear, stake-proportional split with exact integer rational arithmetic [3](#0-2) .

Because these functions are linear (additive) rather than concave (like `sqrt`), splitting stake or voting power across multiple accounts/proposals cannot increase the aggregate payout — `f(a) + f(b) = f(a+b)` for linear `f`, unlike the Sherlock report's `sqrt(a) + sqrt(b) > sqrt(a+b)` case. The docs even explicitly acknowledge Sybil-style stake splitting as a known, out-of-scope centralization concern, but it does not grant extra funds/rewards under the linear reward formula [4](#0-3) .

No other in-scope, unprivileged-transaction-reachable component (accounts/nonces, receipts/congestion, trie/state-witness, tx admission, JSON-RPC, wallet contract) implements a concave/quadratic payout formula that could be manipulated the way `_getPayout` was in the original report.
