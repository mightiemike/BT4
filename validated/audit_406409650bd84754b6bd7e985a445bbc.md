The reported bug is specific to UniswapV3/SolidlyV3-style concentrated-liquidity AMM math (`liquidity = (usdAmount * currentLiquidity) / balanceOf(pool)` combined with tick-range positions and `sqrtPriceX96` calculations). Searching the chainlink repo, the only "liquidity" concepts present relate to CCIP `LockReleaseTokenPool` operations such as `ProvideLiquidity`/`WithdrawLiquidity` and the corresponding changeset `LockReleaseLiquidityOps`, which simply move token balances for cross-chain rebalancing and are validated against a straightforward balance check [1](#0-0) . There is no tick-based AMM liquidity math, no `sqrtRatioX96`/`getLiquidityForAmounts`-style calculation, and no code path where an unprivileged third party can inflate a "total liquidity" figure to cause a proportional liquidity-minting DoS [2](#0-1) . The CCIP token pool liquidity provisioning is a permissioned rebalancer/admin action gated by MCMS/authority checks, not an open AMM pool anyone can deposit into [3](#0-2) , so the root cause and attack path of the reported bug (unbalanced third-party LP deposits skewing a liquidity ratio formula) do not have a reachable analog in this codebase.
