Based on my investigation, I found the strongest single-transaction analog in `collateral-remove-redeem` in `market.clar` — a multi-step entry point pattern similar to the report's "expose an internal step to an external caller" bug class, but here the risk is the opposite: a value computed and validated in step 1 is *reused unchecked* in step 2 after the source has moved.

### Title
Stale `amount` reused for zToken redemption after collateral removal masks share/underlying mismatch - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`, function `collateral-remove-redeem`)

### Summary
`collateral-remove-redeem` performs a two-step operation: (1) call `collateral-remove` to pull `amount` zTokens (shares) out of the user's collateral position into the market contract, then (2) call `vault-redeem` with the *same* `amount` value, treating it as the number of zToken shares to burn for underlying.

### Finding Description [1](#0-0) 

Step 1 (`collateral-remove`) validates health and removes `amount` units of the zToken (a share-denominated asset) from the user's collateral ledger, sending the shares to the market contract. Step 2 (`vault-redeem`) is then called with that identical `amount` as the number of shares to burn. This is correct *only* as long as the unit of `amount` used for the collateral bookkeeping in `market-vault.clar` and the unit expected by `vault-redeem` (shares) stay perfectly aligned across the call boundary — i.e., the "amount" that was health-checked as collateral notional value in step 1 is blindly reused in step 2 without re-deriving it from the actual zToken balance the market received. [2](#0-1)  shows that `collateral-remove`'s health check is computed against `collateral-value`/`debt-value` derived from `get-notional-evaluation`, which itself depends on `accrue-and-cache` — the market's per-block index/lindex cache for the zToken's underlying vault. That cache is populated once for the whole transaction. `vault-redeem` (called in step 2) performs its own internal `total-assets-preview`/index-based conversion for the withdrawal, independent of the market's cached notional evaluation. There is no re-validation step between step 1 and step 2 confirming that the `amount` of shares actually received by the market equals the `amount` requested — the code assumes `contract-call? .v0-market-vault collateral-remove` always sends exactly `amount` and that this is fungible 1:1 with what `vault-redeem` expects.

### Impact Explanation
If `amount` diverges from the actual shares transferred (e.g. due to any transfer-hook, rounding pathway, or future zToken implementation that doesn't guarantee an exact 1:1 transfer), the market would attempt to redeem more/fewer shares than it actually holds, either reverting (temporary freeze of the withdrawal) or, in a divergent implementation, redeeming a stale amount that doesn't correspond to what was actually removed from the user's position — a mismatch between the collateral ledger update and the actual token movement. This lands in the temporary freezing of funds category since a mismatched `amount` would cause `vault-redeem`'s `ERR-INSUFFICIENT-BALANCE` to revert the entire withdrawal atomically, and in the case of any accounting drift, could freeze the user's ability to withdraw their share-collateral through this composed entry point.

### Likelihood Explanation
Low-to-moderate under current SIP-010 zToken implementation (which is a straightforward 1:1 transfer), but the code contains no defensive re-read of the market's actual zToken balance/allowance before step 2, relying purely on the two steps producing identical amounts. Any future ztoken with transfer fees, rebasing, or non-1:1 accounting — or a bug in `market-vault.clar`'s `remove-user-collateral`/`send-tokens` path — would silently break this invariant since Clarity evaluates the two `try!` calls sequentially with no intermediate balance check.

### Recommendation
After step 1 (`collateral-remove`), read the market contract's actual zToken balance delta (or have `collateral-remove` return the exact amount transferred) and pass that verified value into `vault-redeem`, rather than reusing the caller-supplied `amount` across both steps.

### Proof of Concept
1. User calls `collateral-remove-redeem(ft, amount, min-underlying, receiver, price-feeds)`.
2. `collateral-remove` executes, health-checks the position using cached notional values, and calls `market-vault.collateral-remove` which sends `amount` zTokens to the market contract via `send-tokens`.
3. Immediately, `vault-redeem(underlying-id, amount, min-underlying, funds-receiver)` is called, assuming the market now holds exactly `amount` zTokens.
4. If the actual shares received differ from `amount` (e.g., due to a future fee-on-transfer zToken or an off-by-rounding discrepancy in the vault ledger), `vault-redeem`'s internal share-burn (`ft-burn? zft amount account`) either reverts on insufficient balance or burns an incorrect amount unrelated to what was actually removed from the user's collateral position, decoupling the collateral ledger from the actual token flow within a single atomic transaction. (define-public (collateral-remove (ft <ft-trait>) (amount uint) (receiver (optional principal)) (price-feeds (optional (list 3 (buff 8192)))))
  (let ((ft-address (contract-of ft))
        (asset (try! (get-asset ft-address)))
        (asset-id (get id asset))
        (account contract-caller)
        (collateral-receiver (match receiver recv recv contract-caller))
        (position (try! (get-position account)))
        (has-debt (> (len (get debt position)) u0)))

    (asserts! (> amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (if has-debt
        ;; HAS DEBT: Full flow with price resolution and health checks
        (let ((is-collateral-enabled (get collateral asset))
              (feeds-check (try! (write-feeds price-feeds)))
              (position-mask (get mask position))
              (pos-full (if is-collateral-enabled position (try! (get-full-position account))))
              (u-debt (accrue-user-debts (get debt pos-full)))
              (u-coll (accrue-user-collateral (get collateral pos-full)))
              (assets (get-assets position-mask))
              (curr-coll-aid (find-collateral-amount (get collateral position) asset-id))
              (removing-all (is-eq amount curr-coll-aid))
              (current-group (try! (get-egroup position-mask)))
              (current-ltvb (buff-to-uint-be (get LTV-BORROW current-group)))
              (notional-valued-assets (get-notional-evaluation { position: position, assets: assets }))
              (collateral-value (get collateral notional-valued-assets))
              (debt-value (get debt notional-valued-assets))
              (removed-asset-value (find-and-resolve-asset-value assets asset-id amount true)))

          (asserts! (is-healthy collateral-value debt-value current-ltvb) ERR-UNHEALTHY)
          (asserts!
            (if is-collateral-enabled
                (let ((t (asserts! (>= collateral-value removed-asset-value) ERR-INSUFFICIENT-COLLATERAL))
                      (post-removal-collateral-value (- collateral-value removed-asset-value)))
                  (if removing-all
                      (let ((future-mask (bit-and position-mask (bit-not (pow u2 asset-id)))))
                        (try! (is-healthy-with-mask post-removal-collateral-value debt-value future-mask)))
                      (is-healthy post-removal-collateral-value debt-value current-ltvb)))
                (let ((oracle-data (get oracle asset))
                      (price (unwrap! (price-resolve oracle-data) ERR-DISABLED-COLLATERAL-PRICE-FAILED))
                      (decimals (get decimals asset))
                      (user-amount (find-collateral-amount (get collateral pos-full) asset-id))
                      (disabled-notional (normalize (* user-amount price) decimals false))
                      (removal-notional (normalize (* amount price) decimals true))
                      (total-collateral-value (+ collateral-value disabled-notional)))
                  (asserts! (>= total-collateral-value removal-notional) ERR-INSUFFICIENT-COLLATERAL)
                  (is-healthy (- total-collateral-value removal-notional) debt-value current-ltvb)))
            ERR-UNHEALTHY)

          (let ((result (try! (contract-call? .v0-market-vault collateral-remove account amount ft asset-id collateral-receiver))))

(define-public (collateral-remove-redeem (ft <ft-trait>) (amount uint) (min-underlying uint) (receiver (optional principal)) (price-feeds (optional (list 3 (buff 8192)))))
  (let ((ft-address (contract-of ft))
        (asset (try! (get-asset ft-address)))
        (ztoken-id (get id asset))
        (underlying-id (if (is-eq ztoken-id zSTX) STX
                       (if (is-eq ztoken-id zsBTC) sBTC
                       (if (is-eq ztoken-id zstSTX) stSTX
                       (if (is-eq ztoken-id zUSDC) USDC
                       (if (is-eq ztoken-id zUSDH) USDH
                       (if (is-eq ztoken-id zstSTXbtc) stSTXbtc
                       u100)))))))  ;; invalid sentinel for non-ztoken
        (funds-receiver (match receiver recv recv contract-caller)))

    (asserts! (<= underlying-id stSTXbtc) ERR-UNKNOWN-VAULT)
    
    ;; Step 1: Remove collateral - sends zTokens to THIS contract (market)
    ;; receiver=current-contract so market holds the zTokens
    (try! (collateral-remove ft amount (some current-contract) price-feeds))
    
    ;; Step 2: Redeem zTokens for underlying
    ;; vault-redeem calls vault.redeem which burns shares from contract-caller (market)
    ;; Since market now holds the zTokens, this succeeds
    ;; Underlying tokens are sent to the specified receiver
    (vault-redeem underlying-id amount min-underlying funds-receiver)))
