### Title
Gas Key With `FunctionCall` Permission Cannot Withdraw Balance Above `MAX_BALANCE_TO_BURN`, Permanently Locking Funds - (File: `runtime/runtime/src/access_keys.rs`)

### Summary

The `GasKeys` feature introduces a deletion guard: `delete_gas_key` rejects `DeleteKey` when the gas key's balance exceeds `GasKeyInfo::MAX_BALANCE_TO_BURN` (1 NEAR). The only protocol-provided escape hatch is `WithdrawFromGasKey`. However, `WithdrawFromGasKey` is a non-`FunctionCall` action, so it can only be submitted by a transaction signed with a `FullAccess` or `GasKeyFullAccess` key. An account that holds only a `GasKeyFunctionCall` key has no signing path to submit `WithdrawFromGasKey`, and therefore cannot reduce the balance below the threshold. The gas key becomes permanently undeletable and its balance permanently unrecoverable — a direct loss-of-funds outcome reachable by ordinary user actions.
