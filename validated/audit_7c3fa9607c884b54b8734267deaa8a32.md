The reported bug class depends on an operator-bond/relayer-pod mechanism (an actor that stakes a bond, executes cross-chain "jobs" with a user-supplied `gasLimit`, and is permanently locked out — losing its bond — if that gas limit exceeds what a block can support). Java-tron has no equivalent bonded-operator/job-execution construct anywhere in its transaction, actuator, TVM, or consensus code. The closest concepts are:

- `feeLimit`/`energyLimit` handling in `VMActuator.create()`/`call()`, which already caps `feeLimit` at `getMaxFeeLimit()` and derives `energyLimit` from the caller's frozen/available energy — there's no way for a caller to force an unbounded, unpaid gas parameter analogous to the report.
- `Program.spendEnergy`/`checkCPUTimeLimit`, which cleanly throw `OutOfEnergyException`/`OutOfTimeException` when a transaction's execution exceeds its limits — this only fails that single transaction (its executing witness moves on to the next tx/block) and does not lock out or slash any bonded party.

There is no bonded "operator"/"pod" actor in java-tron whose funds or future participation get locked by an attacker-chosen oversized gas/energy parameter, so this bug class has no reachable, unprivileged analog in the codebase.
