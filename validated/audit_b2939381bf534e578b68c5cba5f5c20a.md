### Title
Contract owner (`origin`) is forced to pay energy fees at an unpredictable, unbounded `ENERGY_FEE` (SUN-per-energy) rate set at execution time, with no cap on the actual TRX cost — ([File: chainbase/src/main/java/org/tron/core/capsule/ReceiptCapsule.java])

### Summary
The Infinity Exchange finding describes a buyer who agrees to a maker order without capping the gas *price*, so a third-party executor can settle the order at any `tx.gasprice`, forcing the buyer to reimburse an unpredictable, uncapped TRX cost. The same design pattern exists in java-tron's smart-contract "origin" energy-sponsorship mechanism: a contract owner can pre-fund calls made by arbitrary, anonymous callers (`consume_user_resource_percent` / `origin_energy_limit`), but the amount they actually pay per call is `usage * sunPerEnergy`, where `sunPerEnergy` is the network-wide `ENERGY_FEE` governed by committee proposals and can change between the time the owner configures the sponsorship and the time any anonymous user triggers the contract.

### Finding Description
A contract deployer configures sponsorship of caller energy costs via `UpdateSettingContract` (`consume_user_resource_percent`) and `UpdateEnergyLimitContract` (`origin_energy_limit`), which only bounds the *energy units* the owner will pay, not the TRX cost: [1](#0-0) 

When any account (fully unprivileged, anonymous) later calls the contract via `TriggerSmartContract`, `TransactionTrace.pay()` computes `originEnergyLimit` from the stored contract capsule and hands it to `ReceiptCapsule.payEnergyBill`: [2](#0-1) 

`payEnergyBill` then charges the origin account `usage * sunPerEnergy`, where `sunPerEnergy` is read live from `dynamicPropertiesStore.getEnergyFee()` at the time the transaction executes — not at the time the owner configured `origin_energy_limit`: [3](#0-2) 

`ENERGY_FEE` is a dynamic, committee-adjustable chain parameter that can be changed at any time through `ProposalService`, and there is no owner-configurable cap on this price: [4](#0-3) 

Thus the contract owner's only lever is `origin_energy_limit` (an energy-unit ceiling), exactly analogous to the maker buyer having no `maxGasCost` — they can cap "how much energy" but never "how much it will cost in TRX," and that cost is determined by a third party (the committee/network) at the unpredictable future moment an anonymous caller decides to trigger the contract.

### Impact Explanation
If `ENERGY_FEE` rises (via a passed committee proposal) between the time a contract owner sets a given `origin_energy_limit`/`consume_user_resource_percent`, and the time users trigger the contract, the owner is forced to pay proportionally more TRX per call than they budgeted for, with no on-chain mechanism to cap the TRX cost. Since callers are unprivileged/anonymous and can freely trigger the contract as often as they like, this can materially and repeatedly drain the origin account's balance at whatever the current network-wide energy price is, mirroring the "buyer forced to reimburse gas cost at any tx.gasprice" bug class.

### Likelihood Explanation
`ENERGY_FEE` changes are infrequent and governed by committee proposals rather than an adversarial actor's direct control, and the actual amount charged is still bounded by `originEnergyLimit` (energy units) and the owner's balance, so a sudden fee change is the trigger rather than an attacker directly manipulating price like the trusted `MATCH_EXECUTOR` in the original report. The scenario is realistic but requires a legitimate protocol parameter change plus normal, unprivileged contract usage, which is a lower-likelihood but plausible occurrence.

### Recommendation
Consider allowing contract owners to specify a maximum TRX cost (analogous to `maxGasCost`) they are willing to sponsor per call/period, in addition to (or instead of) the pure energy-unit `origin_energy_limit`, so that a rise in `ENERGY_FEE` cannot silently increase the TRX drain rate beyond what the owner budgeted. Alternatively, snapshot/cap the effective `sunPerEnergy` used against sponsor accounts to a value the owner has explicitly acknowledged.

### Proof of Concept
1. Contract owner deploys/updates a contract with `consume_user_resource_percent < 100` and a given `origin_energy_limit` (via `UpdateEnergyLimitContractActuator.execute`), budgeting for the current `ENERGY_FEE`.
2. A committee proposal changes `ENERGY_FEE` (`ProposalService` `ENERGY_FEE` case) to a much higher value.
3. Any anonymous account calls the contract with `TriggerSmartContract`; `TransactionTrace.pay()` -> `ReceiptCapsule.payEnergyBill` charges the origin account `originUsage * newSunPerEnergy`, which is larger than the owner ever agreed to per unit of energy, and repeatable by any caller with no cap on cumulative TRX cost.
