### Title
Incorrect pro-rata `transferUsage` calculation in `UnDelegateResourceProcessor`/`UnDelegateResourceActuator` can corrupt receiver resource usage accounting - (File: actuator/src/main/java/org/tron/core/vm/nativecontract/UnDelegateResourceProcessor.java)

### Summary
When undelegating BANDWIDTH/ENERGY resources, the amount of "usage" to remove from the receiver account (`transferUsage`) is computed pro-rata to the ratio of `unDelegateBalance` (this particular delegator's share being withdrawn) over the receiver's *aggregate* `getAllFrozenBalanceForBandwidth()`/`getAllFrozenBalanceForEnergy()` (the sum of frozen balance contributed by potentially many different delegators plus the receiver's own frozen balance), while the usage being reduced (`receiverCapsule.getEnergyUsage()`/`getNetUsage()`) is a single aggregate counter not attributable to any specific delegator. This mirrors the reported bug class: a shared/aggregate quantity is reduced using a share computed from one metric (this delegator's contribution to total frozen balance) while the underlying resource was consumed/allocated according to a different, unrelated metric (actual usage pattern of the receiver, and the global `totalEnergyCurrentLimit/totalEnergyWeight` ratio used for the `unDelegateMaxUsage` cap).

### Finding Description
In `UnDelegateResourceProcessor.execute()` (and the duplicated logic in `UnDelegateResourceActuator`), for `ENERGY`: [1](#0-0) 

the code computes:
- `unDelegateMaxUsage`, capped by the receiver's overall energy limit ratio (`totalEnergyCurrentLimit / totalEnergyWeight`), a global-network metric.
- `transferUsage = receiverCapsule.getEnergyUsage() * (unDelegateBalance / receiverCapsule.getAllFrozenBalanceForEnergy())`, a per-delegation-relationship pro-rata share of the receiver's *entire* current energy usage, based purely on how much frozen balance is being withdrawn relative to the receiver's total frozen balance (which can be contributed by multiple different owners/delegators, plus the receiver's own frozen balance).

The receiver's aggregate `energyUsage` counter, however, does not track which delegator's frozen balance "caused" which portion of usage — usage is a single shared number. When multiple owners have delegated resources to the same receiver and undelegate at different times, each undelegation independently subtracts a `transferUsage` computed from the *current* `getAllFrozenBalanceForEnergy()` and *current* `getEnergyUsage()`, which is exactly the same "reduce a shared total by a share computed from a different distribution metric" pattern as the CDS bug (share of deposits vs. share of realized liquidation). This can result in `newEnergyUsage = receiverCapsule.getEnergyUsage() - transferUsage` going negative or in energy/bandwidth usage bookkeeping diverging from real consumption, because the pro-rata subtraction is not reconciled against the true attribution of usage to each delegator's contribution. [2](#0-1) 

This logic is reachable by any account through a `UnDelegateResourceContract`/native TVM `unDelegateResource` call (broadcastable transaction) and does not require privileged access.

### Impact Explanation
Corruption of `energyUsage`/`netUsage` accounting on the receiver account leads to incorrect resource-limit calculations for that account, which can allow the receiver to consume more or less TVM resources than it is entitled to (resource accounting corruption), and in edge cases produce a negative usage value stored to disk that skews global chain-wide resource statistics (`getBlockEnergyUsage`, adaptive energy).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a specific but realistic setup: a receiver account that has resources delegated to it by multiple distinct owners and that also self-consumes energy, followed by sequential `unDelegateResource` calls from different owners. This is achievable by any user through normal TVM-freeze-v2/delegate-resource flows without special privileges, but requires multi-owner delegation scenarios to manifest a meaningfully divergent (incorrect) subtraction, making it a moderate-likelihood, unprivileged-path issue rather than a trivially triggerable one.

### Recommendation
Recompute `transferUsage` for undelegation based on an attribution mechanism that ties usage back to specific delegated balances (e.g., snapshotting usage per delegation relationship, or recalculating usage strictly from the global `totalEnergyCurrentLimit/totalEnergyWeight` ratio applied consistently rather than mixing it with a per-delegation frozen-balance ratio), and clamp `newEnergyUsage`/`newNetUsage` to a minimum of `0` before persisting, mirroring the sherlock report's suggested mitigation of using a single, consistent metric for both the "share" and the "amount actually available/removed" calculations.

### Proof of Concept
1. Account `R` receives delegated ENERGY from owner `A` (large frozen balance) and owner `B` (small frozen balance), and also freezes some energy itself.
2. `R` consumes energy, incrementing `energyUsage`.
3. Owner `B` calls `unDelegateResource` with a small `unDelegateBalance`; `transferUsage` is computed as `R.getEnergyUsage() * (B_unDelegateBalance / R.getAllFrozenBalanceForEnergy())`, subtracted from `R.energyUsage`.
4. Owner `A` subsequently calls `unDelegateResource` for its full share; the same formula recomputes `transferUsage` against the now-reduced `getAllFrozenBalanceForEnergy()` and possibly already-reduced `getEnergyUsage()`, producing a `transferUsage` that does not correspond to `A`'s actual causal share of consumption, and can drive `newEnergyUsage` below the real remaining usage or negative, corrupting `R`'s resource accounting.
