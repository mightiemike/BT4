## Analysis

The premise conflates two independent subsystems that don't share state:

**1. `BalanceCollector` is disconnected from consensus/hashing.** `collect_pre_balances`/`collect_post_balances` in `svm/src/transaction_balances.rs` read account state purely from the SVM's `AccountLoader` in-memory cache during transaction processing [1](#0-0) , and the resulting `BalanceCollector` is only used to populate `TransactionBalancesSet` for `TransactionStatusSender`/RPC responses [2](#0-1) . Nothing here feeds `accounts_lt_hash` or `Bank::hash_internal_state`; even a hypothetical staleness in `BalanceCollector` would only produce wrong RPC-reported pre/post balances, not bank-hash divergence.

**2. `accounts_lt_hash` is computed synchronously per-store, not deferred to freeze.** For every account store, `enqueue_on_chain_accounts_lt_hash_updates` immediately loads `prev_account` via `load_with_fixed_root_do_not_populate_read_cache(&self.ancestors, address)` at the moment of the store, in strict transaction-program order, and pairs it with the just-written `curr_account` [3](#0-2) . `AccountsLtHashUpdate::process` then does `mix_out(prev)` / `mix_in(curr)` for that single update [4](#0-3) . `finish_accounts_lt_hash_updates` (called on freeze) only waits for these already-enqueued per-store jobs to complete and mixes their results in — it doesn't re-derive `prev_account` at freeze time [5](#0-4) . So the "prev" and "curr" snapshots for tx1 (zero) and tx2 (recreate) are captured independently and correctly at their respective store times, regardless of what happens to on-disk storage layout afterward.

**3. `clean_accounts` cannot touch the in-progress (unrooted) slot.** `clean_accounts` operates on `max_clean_root_inclusive` and constructs candidates only from `dirty_stores`/`uncleaned_pubkeys` belonging to already-rooted slots [6](#0-5) ; the current bank being built within a single block is not yet rooted (rooting happens via `add_root`/`squash`, well after freeze), so clean cannot purge any storage entry the attacker writes/zeroes/recreates within that same slot before freeze. Only versions from *previous* rooted slots for that same pubkey could be reclaimed, and that reclaim path removes duplicate/older index entries, not the current-slot value the lt-hash update already captured by reference at store time.

Because the lt-hash `prev_account`/`curr_account` pair is captured eagerly, in-order, at store time — completely independent of physical storage layout and unaffected by background clean of older rooted slots — there is no window in which `clean_accounts` can desynchronize `AccountsLtHashUpdate`'s snapshot from what was actually committed. The scenario described requires an interaction between `BalanceCollector` and lt-hash/clean timing that does not exist in this codebase; they are unrelated subsystems.
