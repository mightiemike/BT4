### Title
Gas-abstraction auto-swap deposit uses a single manipulable AMM quote with fixed slippage, letting the depositor bias the mint price - (File: x/uexecutor/keeper/execute_inbound_gas.go)

### Summary
`ExecuteInboundGas` (the flow that turns a `GAS`-type inbound into a PRC20→WPC swap for the recipient's UEA) prices the swap using a single live call to the UniswapV3-style Quoter contract, then executes the actual swap immediately afterward with a hard-coded 5% slippage band. There is no TWAP, no staleness check, and no protection against the underlying pool's spot price being pushed away from its equilibrium value in the same block the inbound is processed, so value that should accrue to the WPC/PRC20 pool (and its LPs/protocol) can instead be captured by whoever controls the timing of the pool state at execution.

### Finding Description
`ExecuteInboundGas` performs, in sequence, within one keeper call:
1. `GetUniversalCoreQuoterAddress` / `GetUniversalCoreWPCAddress` / `GetDefaultFeeTierForToken`
2. `GetSwapQuote(sdkCtx, quoterAddr, prc20AddressHex, wpcAddr, fee, amount)` — a live `eth_call`-style read against the on-chain Uniswap V3 quoter, which is a spot-price computation over the current pool reserves.
3. `minPCOut := quote * 95 / 100` — a fixed, universal 5% slippage tolerance regardless of pool depth or volatility.
4. `CallPRC20DepositAutoSwap(sdkCtx, prc20AddressHex, ueaAddr, amount, fee, minPCOut)` — the actual swap+deposit, executed against whatever the pool state is at that same point in the block. [1](#0-0) 

This is the same bug class as the Pyth report: a price feed that is fetched and then immediately consumed to determine how many "shares" (here, WPC/PC) a depositor receives, protected only by a static slippage percentage rather than a manipulation-resistant price source (TWAP, signed oracle, or per-block price cap). Because Push Chain is an EVM-compatible chain and the WPC/PRC20 pool lives in the same execution environment as ordinary user transactions, an attacker who also controls the source-chain deposit that generates this inbound can, in the same Push Chain block in which the ballot-triggered `ExecuteInboundGas` call lands, submit an ordinary EVM transaction that skews the pool's spot price (e.g., a large one-sided swap) immediately before the module-originated `CallPRC20DepositAutoSwap` executes, then reverse the skew afterward. The 5% band is wide enough on illiquid or newly-seeded pools to make this profitable, and there is no atomic binding between the quote's freshness and any external validator-attested price — the entire pricing decision rests on manipulable, attacker-adjacent pool state.

### Impact Explanation
Successful manipulation lets an unprivileged, ordinary user extract value from the WPC/PRC20 pool during the mint step of the gas-abstraction inbound flow (`FUNDS`/`GAS`/`GAS_AND_PAYLOAD` inbounds routed through `ExecuteInboundGas`), corrupting PRC20/native asset accounting for the deposit and misrouting value away from the pool/protocol to the attacker — matching the in-scope "corruption of PRC20 or native asset accounting" and "unauthorized module-originated EVM execution" impact classes.

### Likelihood Explanation
Exploitability depends on pool liquidity depth and the attacker's ability to land a same-block manipulation transaction ahead of the module's `CallPRC20DepositAutoSwap` call. On thinly-liquidified WPC/PRC20 pools (most likely at initial launch, before the sponsor's own permissionless-LP hardening applies) a swap sized to move the quoter's spot price by close to 5% is inexpensive, making this a realistic griefing/arbitrage vector for an unprivileged user. I was unable to fully inspect `GetSwapQuote`'s exact quoter call parameters or whether `CallPRC20DepositAutoSwap`'s on-chain contract enforces any independent TWAP check beyond the passed `minPCOut`, so likelihood should be validated against the actual `UniversalCore`/Quoter Solidity contracts, which are outside the indexed Go code.

### Recommendation
Replace or supplement the single spot-price quoter read with a manipulation-resistant reference (TWAP over multiple blocks, or a validator-attested/ballot-based price similar to `chain_meta`), scale the slippage tolerance to pool depth/volatility instead of a fixed 5%, and/or add a maximum price-impact check comparing the pre-swap quote to a longer-window average before allowing `CallPRC20DepositAutoSwap` to proceed.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual (not fully verified against the Solidity contracts, since they are outside the Go-only index):
1. Attacker seeds a source-chain gas-deposit inbound for `GAS`/`GAS_AND_PAYLOAD` targeting their own UEA.
2. In the Push Chain block where the honest validators' ballot finalizes and `ExecuteInboundGas` is scheduled to run, attacker submits an ordinary EVM transaction that swaps a large amount into/out of the WPC/PRC20 pool to skew the Quoter's spot price in their favor.
3. `GetSwapQuote` at line 135 reads the skewed spot price; `minPCOut` at lines 144-145 is computed from that skewed quote with only 5% tolerance.
4. `CallPRC20DepositAutoSwap` at line 148 executes the deposit+swap at the skewed price, minting more WPC/PC to the attacker's UEA than the pool's equilibrium price would justify.
5. Attacker reverses their manipulating swap in the same or a subsequent transaction, realizing a profit funded by the pool/protocol. [2](#0-1)
